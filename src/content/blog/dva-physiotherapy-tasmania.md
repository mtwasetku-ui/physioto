# DVA HOME PHYSIOTHERAPY FUNDING — TASMANIA

## DVA-Funded Home Physiotherapy for Veterans in Tasmania

By Micheal Ghattas, DPT | AHPRA Registered Physiotherapist | 15+ Years Experience

Physio to Home, Tasmania | Last reviewed: July 2026

The Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) funds physiotherapy for eligible veterans and their dependants through the DVA Health Card system. For veterans in Tasmania — particularly those managing chronic pain, mobility limitations, or complex health needs — home-based physiotherapy is a practical way to use this entitlement without the burden of travelling to a clinic.

Physio to Home accepts DVA Gold and White Card holders and can bring physiotherapy directly to your home anywhere across Tasmania.

This guide explains how DVA physiotherapy funding works, which card types are covered, and what to expect from a home visit.

### Who this guide is for

This article is for veterans, war widows and widowers, and their families in Tasmania who want to understand how physiotherapy can be funded through a DVA Health Card, and whether home-based delivery is right for them.

## Does DVA Cover Physiotherapy?

Yes. DVA funds physiotherapy through the Veteran Card system, which has two main levels of cover:

**Gold Card (All Conditions)** — covers physiotherapy that is clinically necessary for any condition, regardless of whether it's related to your service. This is the broadest level of cover, giving Gold Card holders access to physiotherapy for the full range of musculoskeletal, neurological, and general health needs.

**White Card (Specific Conditions)** — covers physiotherapy for conditions DVA has formally accepted as related to your service. If your condition is on your accepted list, physiotherapy for it is funded the same way as for Gold Card holders. It's worth checking which conditions are listed on your file before booking, so your visit is covered correctly.

In both cases, DVA pays the treating physiotherapist directly for eligible treatment — there is no out-of-pocket cost to you.

## How DVA-Funded Physiotherapy Works

**1. A referral is generally required.** Physiotherapy under DVA arrangements typically needs a referral from your GP or specialist before treatment begins. If you're not sure what you currently have in place, we can talk you through it when you contact us.

**2. Your physiotherapist needs to be DVA-registered.** Only physiotherapists registered with DVA can treat and bill Veteran Card holders directly. Physio to Home accepts DVA clients — contact us to confirm current arrangements for your specific situation.

**3. Treatment is organised in cycles.** DVA-funded physiotherapy is typically delivered in structured treatment cycles, which allow your progress to be reviewed and further treatment approved if it's still clinically needed.

**4. You can choose how you're treated.** DVA-funded physiotherapy can be delivered in a clinic, at home, or via telehealth, depending on what's clinically appropriate for you. For veterans with reduced mobility, chronic pain, or transport difficulties, home visits are often the most practical option.

## What Can DVA-Funded Home Physiotherapy Help With?

Physio to Home provides DVA-funded physiotherapy for a wide range of conditions common among veterans, including:

**Musculoskeletal injuries and chronic pain** — old service-related injuries, joint pain, and chronic conditions affecting movement and daily function.

**Post-surgical rehabilitation** — recovery following joint replacement, spinal surgery, or other orthopaedic procedures.

**Falls prevention and balance** — particularly relevant for older veterans, or those with service-related injuries affecting stability.

**Age-related conditions** — osteoarthritis, osteoporosis, and general mobility decline.

**Neurological conditions** — including stroke recovery and Parkinson's disease, where applicable.

Treatment is tailored to your accepted conditions (White Card) or your full clinical picture (Gold Card), and to the goals that matter most to you — whether that's walking further, managing pain, or maintaining independence at home.

## Why Home Visits Suit Many DVA Clients

Many veterans accessing DVA-funded physiotherapy are older, managing chronic pain, or dealing with mobility limitations that make travelling to a clinic difficult. Home-based physiotherapy offers:

- **No travel burden** — particularly valuable for veterans with chronic pain, fatigue, or transport difficulties
- **Assessment in your real environment** — your physiotherapist sees the stairs, bathroom, and layout you actually manage every day, not a clinic room
- **Privacy and comfort** — some veterans prefer the privacy of their own home
- **Consistency** — regular visits that don't depend on someone else driving you to appointments

DVA fully supports home-based physiotherapy delivery where it's clinically appropriate.

## What to Expect: Your First DVA Home Physiotherapy Visit

**1 Confirming your eligibility**

Before your first visit, we confirm your card type (Gold or White), your referral, and — for White Card holders — which conditions are accepted on your file.

**2 Clinical assessment in your home (20–25 minutes)**

A thorough assessment of your movement, strength, pain, and function, taking your service history and current conditions into account.

**3 Goal-setting**

We agree on clear, practical goals — whether that's reducing pain, improving a specific movement, or maintaining the independence you have now.

**4 Initial treatment**

Your first session includes hands-on treatment or supervised exercise, plus the start of a home programme you can continue between visits.

**5 Ongoing review**

As part of your treatment cycle, we track your progress and liaise with your GP as needed to support any further approvals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a referral to see a physiotherapist under DVA?

Generally, yes. A referral from your GP or specialist is typically required before DVA-funded physiotherapy treatment can begin. Contact us if you're not sure what you currently have in place.

### What's the difference between a Gold Card and a White Card for physiotherapy?

A Gold Card covers physiotherapy for any condition. A White Card covers physiotherapy only for the specific conditions DVA has formally accepted as related to your service. Checking your accepted conditions list before booking helps make sure your visit is covered.

### Will I have to pay anything out of pocket?

No. When you see a DVA-registered physiotherapist for eligible treatment, DVA is billed directly and there is no cost to you.

### Can I get DVA-funded physiotherapy at home instead of at a clinic?

Yes. DVA supports home-based physiotherapy where it's clinically appropriate, and many veterans — particularly those with mobility limitations or chronic pain — find this the most practical option.

### How do I find out if Physio to Home can see me under DVA?

Contact us directly with your card type and we'll confirm eligibility and what's needed before your first visit.
