## Home physiotherapy in Devonport

For people living in **Devonport**, a home visit means your physiotherapist can work with you in the environment where you need to move every day. This may include practising safe transfers, getting up from a chair or bed, managing steps, improving walking confidence or planning exercises around your home.

Depending on your assessment and goals, physiotherapy may help with:

- Back, neck, shoulder, hip or knee pain.
- Reduced strength, balance or walking confidence.
- Rehabilitation after an operation or hospital stay.
- Falls prevention and safer movement around the home.
- Neurological conditions that affect movement and daily activities.
- Maintaining mobility and independence as you get older.

If you live outside central Devonport, tell us your suburb or town when you enquire. We will check the address and current travel capacity rather than assume every surrounding locality is covered.

## What can a home visit physiotherapist help with?

The right treatment depends on your symptoms, health history and goals. Our [physiotherapy services](/services) include support for a range of conditions and rehabilitation needs.

### Back, neck and joint pain

Assessment and an individualised plan may help improve movement, strength and confidence with daily activities. Treatment is tailored to your presentation and may include exercise, education and practical strategies for managing symptoms.

### Post-surgery and post-hospital rehabilitation

After an operation or hospital stay, a physiotherapist can assess your current mobility and help you work towards appropriate recovery goals. Your plan will reflect any post-operative instructions and precautions provided by your treating team.

### Falls prevention, balance and mobility

A home assessment can identify movement challenges in the environment where you live. Treatment may include balance activities, strength exercises, walking practice and strategies to make everyday movement safer.

### Neurological rehabilitation

People living with conditions such as stroke or Parkinson's disease may need support with walking, balance, strength and functional activities. A physiotherapist can discuss whether a home-based program is appropriate for their needs.

### Aged care and maintaining independence

For older people, treatment may focus on maintaining mobility, improving confidence and supporting everyday activities. Family members and carers can be involved in discussions where appropriate.

## Funding and payment options

The funding pathway depends on your circumstances, eligibility and the arrangements in place with the relevant funder or provider. Contact us to discuss your situation before booking.

Options may include:

- **Private appointments:** You can contact us directly to discuss private home visits and fees.
- **Private health insurance:** Rebates depend on your policy and insurer. Check your cover and any home-visit conditions with your fund.
- **Medicare:** Eligible patients may be able to access a limited number of allied health services under a GP Chronic Disease Management plan. Ask your GP about eligibility and referral requirements.
- **NDIS:** We can discuss physiotherapy enquiries from NDIS participants and the applicable plan and payment arrangements.
- **DVA:** Eligible veterans can contact us to discuss home physiotherapy and the relevant referral or approval requirements.
- **My Aged Care:** Physiotherapy may be available through Support at Home or CHSP where it is included in the person's approved services and provider arrangements.

For more detail, read our guide to [My Aged Care home physiotherapy funding](/blog/my-aged-care-home-physiotherapy-funding).
